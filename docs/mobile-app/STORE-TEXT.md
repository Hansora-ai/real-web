## Google Play: production access answers (closed test)

1. **How did you recruit testers?**
We used a paid testing service (Testers Community) to reach at least 12 testers on different Android phones, and we also shared the app with a few content creators and small business owners we know.

2. **How easy was it to recruit testers?** Neither easy nor difficult.

3. **Engagement from testers**
Testers installed the app, created images, videos and audio, tried the menus, pricing and history, and looked at the Automation (AI employee) section. They reported no crashes. We also tested the app ourselves every day on Android and iPhone.

4. **Summary of feedback and how it was collected**
Feedback came through the testing service's written report and from our own testing. No crashes or blocking bugs were found. Testers asked for a short introduction for new users, Share and Rate buttons, a longer store description and clearer screenshots. In our own testing we found a music result whose player stayed at 0:00 and could not be paused or moved, and monthly plans that could not be reached on the Automation pricing page.

5. **Intended audience**
Adults and teenagers 13 and over who create content (creators, marketers), and small business owners who want an AI assistant to answer customers on Instagram, WhatsApp, Telegram, TikTok and the phone.

6. **How the app provides value**
One app for the best AI models: videos, images, music and voices from a prompt or a photo, with one credit balance. Businesses get an AI employee that answers customers day and night, takes orders and books appointments, so they miss fewer sales.

7. **Expected installs in the first year:** 10K to 100K.

8. **Changes made based on the closed test**
• Added a 3-screen introduction for new users (with Skip)
• Added "Share Hansora" and "Rate Hansora" in the menu
• Replaced the audio player for music and voice results (it stayed at 0:00 and could not be paused or moved)
• Made the monthly plans reachable on the Automation pricing page
• Added purchases through Google Play Billing (credit packs and monthly plans)
• Made the content safety filter stricter (prompts, uploaded photos and results) and added Report content
• Rewrote the store description and made new screenshots with captions

9. **How did you decide the app is ready?**
After 14 days of closed testing with no crashes, after fixing every issue found during testing, and after testing purchases, sign-in and account deletion on real phones.

10. **What did you do differently this time?** (only if Google asks)
We made the safety filter stricter so sexual requests about people are blocked and results are checked, added Report content, and fixed the issues found in testing before applying.

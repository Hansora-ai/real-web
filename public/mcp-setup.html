FAQ

Questions

Do I need a Hansora account?

Yes. When you connect, you sign in to Hansora and approve access. Generations are paid from that account's balance, and results also appear in your Hansora history.

Does it cost extra?

No. Connecting is free. You only pay normal Hansora credits for what you generate, the same as on the website.

Which apps does it work with?

ChatGPT, Claude (web, desktop and Claude Code), Cursor (app and web) and any other app that lets you add a custom connector. Use the URL https://hansora.co/mcp.

Can I disconnect later?

Yes. Remove the Hansora app or connector in your AI app's settings at any time. Access stops right away.

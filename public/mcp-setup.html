Connect it

  1. In ChatGPT or Claude, open the area for connectors or custom integrations.
  2. Add a custom MCP connector and enter https://hansora.co/mcp.
  3. Sign in to Hansora and approve the connection.
  4. Ask the assistant to list Hansora models, quote a generation, or create an image, video, speech track, or song.

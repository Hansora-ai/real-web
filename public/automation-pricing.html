Pricing for your AI employee

Start small with a credit pack, or take a monthly plan when you are busy: every reply, comment and call costs less.

Monthly plans renew automatically, so they are paid with an international card. With a Russian card, choose a pay-as-you-go pack.

Credits are shared with Hansora Creative – one balance for your whole Hansora account. Prices in USD.

What uses credits

You only pay for what your AI employee actually does. Monthly plans pay less for every action.

WhatsApp template messages (for example "your order is ready" after 24 hours) are billed by Meta directly to your WhatsApp account, not in Hansora credits.

Questions

Pay as you go or monthly?

Pay as you go is best to start: buy a pack once, no subscription. If your AI employee answers many customers every month, a monthly plan gives you credits every month and makes every reply, comment and call cheaper.

What happens when my credits run out?

Your AI employee stops replying automatically and hands new conversations to you, so no customer is ignored. Top up any time and it continues.

Do monthly credits expire?

No. Credits from your plan stay on your balance. The lower prices per reply, comment and call apply while your plan is active.

How many AI employees can I have?

As many as you need, on every option. You pay only for what they do.

Can I cancel a monthly plan?

Yes, any time on this page. Your plan stays active until the end of the month you paid for, and your credits stay on your balance.

Can I pay with a Russian card?

Yes, for pay-as-you-go packs. Choose "Russian card" above. Monthly plans need an international card because they renew automatically.

Where do I see what I used?

Every AI reply and call minute is listed in Usage & credits for each AI employee, and your full credit history is on your profile.

Cancel your plan?

Your plan stays active until the end of this month. Your credits stay on your balance.

Questions

What happens when my credits run out?

Your AI employee stops replying automatically and hands new conversations to you, so no customer is ignored. Top up any time and it continues.

Do I need a subscription?

No. You buy credits when you need them. There is no monthly fee for Hansora Automation.

Where do I see what I used?

Every AI reply and call minute is listed in Usage & credits for each AI employee, and your full credit history is on your profile.

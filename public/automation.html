Questions

Good questions. Clear answers.

What is an AI employee?

An assistant that knows your business — your products, prices, hours and rules — and talks to your customers on Instagram, WhatsApp and the phone using only that information.

Do I need to know anything about AI?

No. You describe your business in plain words, test it in a private chat, then connect your accounts with the official Instagram and WhatsApp sign-in.

Will it make up prices or promises?

No. It only uses the facts you give it. When something is missing or the customer needs a person, it hands the conversation to your team and tells you.

Can it book appointments and take orders?

Yes. It checks your free times, books after the customer confirms, collects order details, and notifies you on WhatsApp and email.

Which languages does it speak?

Many — English, Spanish, French, German, Arabic, Russian, Turkish and more. You choose up to 10, and it replies in the customer’s language.

Can I use my existing Hansora account?

Yes. Sign in with your Hansora account. Your automation data is kept separate from your other Hansora work.

On this page

    This policy explains what information Hansora Automation handles, why, and how you control it. It adds to the general Hansora Privacy Policy. Automation is operated by Individual Entrepreneur Hovhannes Vardanyan (HOVHANNES VARDANYAN VAHEI A/DZ), trading as HANSORA (“Hansora”, “we”), Yerevan, Armenia. Hansora Automation is a separate product from Hansora Creative, our AI image and video tool on the same website; Hansora Creative does not use Instagram or WhatsApp data.

    1. Two kinds of people

    Business owners (our users) create an AI employee and connect their accounts. For their account and business information, Hansora decides how the data is used.

    Their customers are people who message, comment on, or call a business that uses Automation. For customers’ information, Hansora processes data on behalf of that business and following its instructions. The business is responsible for telling its customers how their information is used. If you are a customer of a business, you can contact that business, or us, about your data.

    2. Information we handle

    From business owners

    • Account: your Hansora account details, credit balance and usage (see the general Privacy Policy).
    • Business knowledge: business name, description, products, prices, hours, service areas, FAQs, policies, tone and instructions you enter.
    • Settings: working hours, booking and order rules, handoff rules, phone settings, and notification preferences, including the WhatsApp number and email you choose for alerts.
    • Connected accounts: for Instagram, your professional account ID, username, name and profile picture (shown so you can see which account is connected), and the list of your posts and reels when you choose one for an automation; for WhatsApp, your WhatsApp Business Account ID, phone number ID and display number; for Google Calendar, the calendar you choose. Access tokens are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and are never shown in the browser.

    From a business’s customers (through connected channels)

    • Instagram: messages sent to the business, comments on the business’s posts or reels, the sender’s Instagram-scoped ID and, where available, username, name and profile picture, and timestamps.
    • WhatsApp: messages sent to the business number, the sender’s phone number and profile name, message status (delivered, read), and descriptions of media (for example “sent a photo”).
    • Phone calls: caller number, call time and length. Call audio is processed in real time to run the conversation. If the business keeps transcripts turned on, a text transcript is saved.
    • Actions: details collected to create bookings, orders or leads (for example name, phone number, address, requested service or time).

    From Google Calendar (only if the business connects it)

    • Busy and free times of the chosen calendar, the list of calendars you can write to, and events created by Hansora for bookings.

    3. How we use it

    • to generate and send replies from the business’s connected account, and to show conversations in the business’s inbox;
    • to check availability, create or cancel bookings, record orders and leads, and hand conversations to the business’s team;
    • to notify the business owner (WhatsApp from Hansora’s number, and email);
    • to count usage and deduct credits, and to show the business its usage log;
    • to keep the service secure, prevent abuse, fix problems, and meet legal obligations.

    We do not sell personal data, use customers’ messages for advertising, build marketing profiles from them, or use them to train Hansora’s own AI models.

    4. Instagram and WhatsApp (Meta) data

    Data we receive through Meta’s platforms is used only to provide Automation to the business that connected the account, as described above, and in line with Meta’s Platform Terms and Developer Policies. We do not sell it, share it with data brokers, or use it for advertising. We keep it only as long as needed for the service (see Retention), and delete it when the account is disconnected or deletion is requested. Removing Hansora in Instagram or Meta settings also sends us a deletion request, which we process automatically.

    5. Google Calendar data

    Hansora’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Calendar data is used only to find free times and to create, update or cancel the business’s own booking events. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not read by people except where needed for security or support with your permission, and not used to train AI models.

    6. Service providers we use

    We share information only with providers that help run Automation, each under their own terms:

    • Meta (Instagram, WhatsApp) — to receive and send messages;
    • Google (Gemini) — to generate text replies, either directly or through ElevenLabs, which also stores the business’s knowledge files and turns voice messages into text;
    • Google (Gemini) — to understand photos and videos customers send, to run phone conversations; Google Calendar if connected;
    • LiveKit and the business’s telephone (SIP) provider — to connect calls;
    • Supabase (database) and Netlify (hosting) — to store and run the service;
    • Resend — to send email alerts to business owners;
    • payment providers for credit purchases (see the general Privacy Policy).

    Providers may process data in other countries, mainly Sweden (our database) and the United States. We use providers that commit to protecting data and we send them only what is needed.

    7. How long we keep it

    • Access tokens: deleted immediately when an account is disconnected or removed.
    • Photos, videos and voice messages customers send: a private copy is kept for 30 days so the business can see it in its inbox, then deleted automatically. The text description or transcript stays with the conversation.
    • Conversations, contacts, transcripts, bookings, orders and leads: kept while the business uses Automation, so the inbox and records stay available. The business can delete them; after a deletion request they are removed within 30 days.
    • Business knowledge and settings: kept until the AI employee or account is deleted.
    • Usage and credit records: kept for up to seven years for accounting and tax, as described in the general Privacy Policy.
    • Backups and security logs: removed through normal rotation, usually within 90 days.

    8. Your choices and rights

    • Business owners can edit or delete their business information, disconnect any channel, turn transcripts off, and request deletion of all Automation data.
    • Customers of a business can ask that business, or us, to access or delete their messages and details.
    • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, restrict or delete personal information, and to complain to a data-protection authority.

    See the data deletion page for step-by-step instructions.

    9. Security

    Connections use official sign-in (OAuth). Access tokens are encrypted. Each business can only see its own data, enforced by database access rules. Webhooks from Meta are signature-checked. No system is perfectly secure; please tell us right away if you suspect a problem.

    10. Children

    Automation is for businesses and is not directed at children. Business owners must be at least 18.

    11. Changes

    We may update this policy as Automation changes. We will post the new version here and change the date above.

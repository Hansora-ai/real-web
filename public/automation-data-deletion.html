Data deletion

How to remove Hansora’s access and delete your data.

Last updated: September 28, 2026

On this page

    You can remove Hansora Automation’s access and delete the data it holds at any time. Pick the option that fits you.

    1. Business owners: disconnect in Hansora

    1. Open AI employees and choose your AI employee.
    2. Open the channel (Instagram or WhatsApp) and choose Disconnect.

    Access tokens are deleted immediately and the AI stops replying on that channel. To also delete the conversations, contacts and records from that channel, email us (option 3).

    2. Remove Hansora from Instagram or Facebook

    1. In Instagram, go to Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites (or in Facebook, Settings → Apps and websites, or Business settings → Integrations for WhatsApp).
    2. Find Hansora and choose Remove.

    Meta then notifies us automatically. We delete the access tokens and the Instagram or WhatsApp conversations, contacts and automation history for that account, and give you a confirmation code you can check on this page.

    3. Ask us to delete everything

    Email hansora.ai.bot@gmail.com from the email address of your Hansora account with the subject “Hansora Automation data deletion”. Tell us whether to delete one AI employee or all Automation data. We may ask you to confirm your identity. We complete requests within 30 days and email you when it is done.

    4. Customers of a business

    If you messaged, commented to, or called a business that uses Hansora Automation and want your messages and details deleted, you can ask that business, or email us at hansora.ai.bot@gmail.com with the business name and your Instagram username or phone number. We will delete them within 30 days and let the business know.

    5. What is deleted and what is kept

    • Deleted: access tokens, conversations, messages, contacts, call transcripts, comment automation history, bookings, orders and leads for the account or AI employee.
    • Kept only where required: credit and payment records for accounting and tax (up to seven years), and short-lived security logs. Backups roll off within about 90 days.

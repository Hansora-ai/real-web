How it's counted

  • Money in = what Dodo and YooMoney received, minus tax, refunds and fees. Dodo's fee is its standard 4% + 1.5% (outside US) + $0.40 per payment.
  • Credits sold cost = credits sold that day × what one credit costs on average (measured from buyers' generations of the last 30 days), counted on the day of the sale.
  • Real-use correction = on the days buyers generate: the average cost of the credits they used minus what those generations really cost. Negative when they use thin-margin models (e.g. Seedance 2.5 1080p), positive for cheap ones (Wan, Grok). When a buyer has used everything, their profit is exactly what they paid minus what they really cost.
  • Free users and your own use = the real provider cost of their generations that day.
  • Profit = Money in − Credits sold cost − Free users − Own use − Expenses.
  • Provider spending (grey) = what all generations that day really cost at the providers, for reference.
  • Days are Armenia days, the same as your Dodo dashboard.

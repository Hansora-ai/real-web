Delete your Hansora account

This page explains how to delete your Hansora AI account (the Hansora app and hansora.co use the same account), what is deleted and what is kept.

How to request deletion

Option 1: in the Hansora app

  1. Open the Hansora app and log in.
  2. Open Menu → Profile.
  3. Scroll to Delete account and choose Delete my whole Hansora account (or Delete my Automation data to delete only your AI employee data).
  4. Confirm. You will see that your request was sent.

Option 2: by email (without the app)

  1. Send an email to contact@hansora.org from the email address of your Hansora account.
  2. Subject: Delete my Hansora account. Say whether you want to delete the whole account or only your Automation data.
  3. We reply to confirm. We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours.

We complete every deletion request within 30 days and tell you when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account and profile (name, email address, profile photo, login details).
  • Your creations: generated images, videos and audio, your prompts and generation history.
  • Files you uploaded (photos, videos, audio).
  • Your remaining credits and plans (they cannot be refunded or restored after deletion).
  • Hansora Automation data: AI employees, business knowledge, connected channels and their access tokens, conversations, contacts, orders, bookings and phone settings.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Payment and invoice records (amount, date, product, payment reference) are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires, then deleted.
  • Security and fraud-prevention logs may be kept for up to 90 days.
  • Backups are overwritten in the normal backup cycle, within 30 days.

Subscriptions bought through Apple or Google are managed by them: cancel them in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions so you are not charged again.

Run your AI employee from ChatGPT

Create it, teach it, test it and start AI phone calls by just asking.

Connect in one minute

  1. Open ChatGPT settings

    In ChatGPT settings, find the apps and connectors section and choose to add a custom one. Until Hansora Automation is listed in the ChatGPT app directory, this needs developer mode turned on there.

  2. Paste this address

    Name it “Hansora Automation” and paste the address below.

    https://hansora.co/automation/v2/mcp
  3. Log in to Hansora

    ChatGPT opens the Hansora login. Use the same account as on this site.

  4. Ask anything

    For example: “How is my business doing today?”

What you can ask

Your AI employee

  • “Create an AI employee for my bakery”
  • “Add our new prices to what it knows”
  • “Ask it about delivery, as a customer”

Phone calls

  • “Call +1 555 010 2030 and confirm tomorrow’s appointment”
  • “What happened on today’s calls?”
  • “Call customers to confirm new orders”

Orders and bookings

  • “Show today’s bookings”
  • “Mark order 14 as completed”
  • “Add a product: Mug, $12, 30 in stock”

Day to day

  • “What needs my attention?”
  • “Answer the visitor waiting in my website chat”
  • “When someone comments ‘price’, send them my price list”

Your customers’ privacy

Chats from Instagram, WhatsApp, TikTok, Telegram and Messenger are not sent to ChatGPT. There you see how many chats are waiting and a link to your Hansora inbox. Website chats and phone calls can be read in ChatGPT.

Using the app is free. AI replies, test messages and phone minutes use your credits as usual.
